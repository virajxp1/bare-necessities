Everything operational lives in Convex: the budget ledger, guest list, seating
charts, photo groups, and RSVP reminders. A built-in AI assistant answers
questions in plain language and proposes every change as a plan that a person
has to approve. For the full story, read
[the case study](/blog/how-we-ran-our-wedding-weekend-using-ai/).
