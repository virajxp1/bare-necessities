A hyperlocal, peer-to-peer food marketplace where anyone can be both a buyer
and a seller: timed listings with explicit seller acceptance and atomic
inventory, counteroffers, pickup codes, blind reviews, and moderation tooling.
Launching city-first with a small founding team, each assisted by terminal
coding agents.
